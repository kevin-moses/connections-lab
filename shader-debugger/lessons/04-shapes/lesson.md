# Shapes with distance

In lesson 3 you drew a circle from `length(uv - 0.5)`: every pixel's **distance** to the center. That idea goes a long way. If you can measure how far a pixel is from a shape, you can draw the shape, its outline, a glow around it, or two shapes merged into one, all with the same few tools.

These distance numbers are easiest to understand by looking at them. Use **Inspect values** in **Diverging** mode: blue below zero, red above, a line at zero.
