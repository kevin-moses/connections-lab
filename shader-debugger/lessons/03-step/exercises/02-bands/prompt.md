## Bands

`1.0 - step(edge, x)` flips a step: 1 **below** the edge, 0 above.

Multiplying two 0-or-1 values gives 1 only when **both** are 1. That makes multiplication an AND.

Keep only the stretch between two edges: make a vertical white band from **x = 0.3** to **x = 0.7**. The starter already has the left side.
