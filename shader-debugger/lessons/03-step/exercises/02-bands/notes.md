Key idea: multiply step results to AND them; 1 - step to invert.
Common mistakes: adding instead of multiplying (left + right side gives 1 or 2 everywhere: white); step(uv.x, 0.7) instead of 1 - step(0.7, uv.x) (actually equivalent except exactly at the edge: accept it).
Nudges: ask what `left` is at x = 0.8 and what they need to multiply it by there to get 0.
