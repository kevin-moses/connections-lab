## A rectangle

A vertical band times a horizontal band is a **rectangle**: white only where both bands are white.

Make a white rectangle from **(0.2, 0.3)** to **(0.8, 0.6)**: x from 0.2 to 0.8, and y from 0.3 to 0.6. `bandX` is done. Build `bandY` the same way, then combine the two.

Bonus thought: `min(a, b)` also works as AND for 0/1 values, and `max(a, b)` works as OR. What would `max(bandX, bandY)` draw?
