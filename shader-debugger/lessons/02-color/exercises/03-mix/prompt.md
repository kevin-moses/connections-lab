## Blending with mix

`mix(a, b, t)` blends from `a` (when `t = 0`) to `b` (when `t = 1`). It's p5's `lerpColor()`, and it works on single numbers too.

Make a horizontal gradient from the deep blue `left` on the left edge to the warm orange `right` on the right edge. What value already runs from 0 to 1 across the canvas?
