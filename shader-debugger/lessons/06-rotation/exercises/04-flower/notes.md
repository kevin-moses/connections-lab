Key idea: a polar shape compares r against a radius that depends on the angle; cos(n * a) gives n bumps around the circle.
Common mistakes: cos(a) * 5.0 (no petals, just a shifted circle); comparing a against the edge instead of r; cos(a * 5.0 / 6.28) (degrees confusion, one slow bump); adding 0.08 * cos(...) to r instead of to the edge (flips which parts bulge).
Nudges: ask what the edge radius should be at 0° and at 36°; have them inspect the edge expression in Auto mode and count the bright spokes.
